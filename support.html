Support

Need a hand? We're happy to help.

My photo is rejected as "not compliant" — why?

Passportr checks official requirements. Common reasons: your head isn't straight, your eyes aren't visible (glasses/hair), more than one person is in frame, or the photo is too low-resolution. Retake facing the camera directly, in even light, with a neutral expression.

Which countries are supported?

EU / Schengen, United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, India, China (visa) and Japan — each with the correct size and head proportions. More are on the way.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your iPhone; your photos never leave the device.

I paid but exporting is still locked.

Open the unlock screen and tap "Restore purchase". If it still doesn't unlock, email us with the date of purchase and we'll sort it out.

How do I print my photo?

After unlocking, use "Export / Share" to print or save a single photo, or "Print Sheet" to get a 10×15 cm sheet with multiple copies for a photo lab or home printer.
