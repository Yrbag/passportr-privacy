Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 June 2026

The short version: Passportr does everything on your device. Your photos never leave your iPhone. We don't have accounts, we don't run analytics, and we don't track you.

Photos and camera

Passportr uses your camera and photo library only to create your passport or ID photo. All image processing — background removal, cropping and the compliance check — runs locally on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else, and we never see them.

Data we collect

None. Passportr has no user accounts, collects no personal information, and contains no analytics or advertising SDKs. We do not track your activity across apps or websites.

Purchases

Passportr offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock exporting. Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store; we never receive your payment details. To manage your unlock across devices we use RevenueCat, which processes an anonymous purchase identifier and your App Store receipt. This data is not linked to your identity and is used only to validate your purchase.

Children

Passportr does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
